How Sponsorship Works

  1. Book a Placement

    A fixed creative — image plus link — or an embed snippet. Weight decides how often it appears.

  2. Set Schedule & Caps

    Start and end dates, plus an optional impression cap. Delivery pauses itself when either hits.

  3. Pay for Attention, Not People

    You get aggregate impressions, clicks, and dismissals. Nothing personal — there is nothing personal to give.

  4. Stay Clearly Labelled

    Every unit carries an “Advertisement” tag and a dismiss control. Readers always know what is paid.
