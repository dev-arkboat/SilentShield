How Your Report Travels

  1. You Capture Proof

    A photo or video is required — audio can ride along. Metadata is scrubbed and filenames are randomised before anything is stored.

    No waiting room, no moderator queue. Your letter appears as Awaiting Justice the second you send it.

    A logged-in officer claims your case — and only yours. Nobody else can touch it, and stale claims return to the queue after 10 months.

    Closure needs a written outcome plus photo, video, or audio evidence, published against your case for everyone to inspect.
