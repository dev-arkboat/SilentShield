How You Stay Invisible

Everything below exists for one reason: the wrong people must never be able to work backwards from a report to a human being.

  1. No Account, No Name Field

    The form has nowhere to type who you are — that is deliberate, not missing.

  2. Nothing Identifying Is Stored

    Our intake never reads network identifiers, and our logs are filtered to drop them if they ever appear.

  3. Photo Trails Are Scrubbed

    Location and device metadata is stripped from images, and every filename is replaced with a random code.

  4. Publication Is Instant

    Your letter goes live as Awaiting Justice immediately — there is no back room where names could leak.

  5. Handling Expires

    One officer per case, and cases left hanging return to the public queue after {{ ttl_days }} days (about 10 months).

  6. Guardians Stay Masked

    Marketplace contact runs through our blind relay. Hacker identities are checked by the team and never published.

Your Half of the Bargain

  • Film from a safe distance; leave out faces and plates that are not the story.
  • Do not say your name on camera. Trim it if it slips out.
  • Use a connection you trust — a VPN or Tor on public Wi-Fi.
  • Save your case code (like AC-1A2B3C4D). It is the only thread.
